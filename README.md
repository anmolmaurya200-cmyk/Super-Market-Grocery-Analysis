# Supermart Grocery Sales Analysis

An Excel analysis of 9,994 grocery orders from Jan 2015 to Dec 2018 (Tamil Nadu, India), covering overall performance, products, profit and discounts, and geography.

## Objectives
1. **Overall performance:** total sales and profit, category share, and monthly and yearly trends
2. **Product performance:** top categories and sub-categories, and high-sales / low-margin products
3. **Profit and discount:** highest-profit categories and regions, and the relationship between discount and profit
4. **Geography:** city-level sales and margin comparison

## Key findings
- Total sales of ₹14.96M and profit of ₹3.75M, a **25.05% margin**
- Sales grew from ₹2.98M (2015) to ₹4.98M (2018), a **CAGR of 18.7%**
- Sep, Nov and Dec account for **42% of annual sales**
- **No relationship between discount and profit** (correlation 0.000); margin stays at about 25% in every discount band
- Beverages ranks 7th as a category but holds the top two sub-categories (Health Drinks and Soft Drinks)
- Kanyakumari is the #1 city by sales but earns a below-average margin (24.4%)

## Recommendations
- Plan stock and staffing around the Sep-Dec peak, and run promotions in Jan-Feb
- Test cutting deep discounts in favor of targeted promotions
- Improve margins on high-selling, low-margin lines such as Cookies, Masalas, Cakes and Spices
- Benchmark low-margin cities against the highest-margin ones
